# Funding applications

A published funding call can pin one to ten ordered bilingual survey revisions. The extension creates and attaches them through the agency API. Organization application viewers can preview every published form and read their organization's saved applications. Contributors start and edit one shared draft containing every form; managers review, submit or delete drafts. `admin` alone does not grant application access.

**Apply for funding** lists published calls with agency, program, stream and UTC start/end dates and times. **Start application** creates or resumes the organization's shared draft. Saved applications remain listed even if their call closes or is withdrawn. Draft answer validation, bilingual routing, pages/sections/subsections, branching and optional attachments reuse the same provider and response engine as agreement forms. Preview answers are deliberately separate and never saved.

`POST /api/organizations/:organizationId/funding-calls/:callId/applications` accepts `{locale:'en'|'fr'}` and returns the standard response result. Organization response save/read/check/submit/delete routes apply unchanged. Starting, editing, file mutations, review and submission require a published call in its UTC date-time window, including both boundary instants. The extension supplies `startTime` and `endTime` as `HH:mm` UTC alongside the existing dates. Omitted times preserve all-day behavior for older callers. Migration 010 backfills existing calls from midnight through the final microsecond of the closing day. Application snapshots and exports include both dates and times; older snapshots may omit times. Read access and manager draft deletion remain available after closing/withdrawal.

Migration 005 backfills funding-call agency ancestry and adds an optimistic call revision. Migration 016 adds `funding_call_form` and backfills existing single-form calls. Editing call details or changing its ordered forms advances the revision. Each organization's internally managed application set freezes the call, every form revision and attachment policy. These sets cannot be edited through ordinary government set APIs. Changed call revisions invalidate old drafts; the manager can delete and restart. Withdrawing/republishing an unchanged call preserves draft compatibility. A new draft may follow a final submission.

The government call metadata update requires `expectedRevision` and rejects stale writes. Form attachment changes are barred after the first submitted application, including withdrawn submissions. A new survey revision may still be authored, but the call stays pinned to its prior revision. Previously submitted response snapshots and exports retain their original questions and answers.

Organization transactions lock the organization then the call; government changes serialize on the call row. Authorization, publication, dates and pinned revision are checked again inside sensitive writes. Submitted responses and exports remain immutable. Exports include `application` with portal call/agency/program/stream IDs, source and foreign-system mappings, dates and call revision. Agency submissions include `callId`; integration credentials can retrieve the exports and private files.
